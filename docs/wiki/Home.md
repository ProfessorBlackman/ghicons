# GHIcons Wiki

Welcome to the GHIcons wiki — the central knowledge base for contributors, maintainers, and anyone who wants to understand how the project works.

GHIcons is a React icon library bringing Ghanaian cultural symbols — Adinkra, national icons, and more — into the hands of developers worldwide. It is open source, community-driven, and still growing.

## 🌍 About the Project

Ghana has a rich visual heritage — hundreds of Adinkra symbols, national emblems, and cultural motifs that carry deep meaning. Most icon libraries ignore this entirely.

GHIcons exists to change that. Every symbol added to this library is one more piece of Ghanaian culture that developers can reference, use, and share in their products — whether they're building for a Ghanaian audience or simply want their work to reflect a broader world.

The project is maintained by [Methuselah Nwodobeh](https://github.com/ProfessorBlackman) and built by contributors from the community.
